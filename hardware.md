## Hardware specifications

### Parts List

This project was designed for implementation with cheap and accessible parts, at the cost of spectral resolution.
Most parts for the original project were obtained in a high school physics lab storage, but alternatives can be purchased
from vendors online.

#### Transmissive Grating

The transmissive grating used for this spectrograph was a standard physics lab 1000 lines/mm grating (dimensions fit 
any grating around 50 x 50 x 2 mm in footprint).


#### Lenses 

Based on the geometry of the design, the focal length of the focus lens is 103mm, and the focal length of the collimation
lens is 73mm. 

The focus lens is made for a 50 x 50 x 5 mm footprint lens, and the collimation lens is made for a 25 x 25 x 3.5 mm foorprint lens.

#### Camera

The current camera adapter fits standard 1.25" T mount adapter, available for most DSLR and CCD/CMOS cameras.

PLEASE NOTE: This design was optimized for the ZWO ASI2600MM Pro dimensions and backfocus. You can see details for
the design of this camera and adjust the exact position of a different camera relative to these dimesions: https://www.zwoastro.com/product/new-asi2600mm-mc-pro/ (see "Mechanical Diagram")

#### Fasteners

All of the fasteners are fit for M3 screws:

- 6: M3 x 0.50 x 30
- 6: M3 x 0.50 x 8 (for razor slit)
- 6: M3 x 0.50 nuts
- 2: M3 x 0.50 x 8 (thumbscrews for Camera T Mount)
- Optional for mounting bracket:
    - 4: M3 x 0.50 x 12
    - 4: M3 x 0.50 x [any length, depending on mounting surface]

Note that without the mounting bracket, the weight of the camera will most likely make the spectrograph uneven on a flat surface.

#### Razor slit 

The slit for this spectrograph was designed to work with standard shaving razor blades. The optimal width of the slit is 25 micrometers,
but this does not (and will likely not) be exact.
